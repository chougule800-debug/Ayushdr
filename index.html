About AFI Belagavi

Know more about the AYUSH Federation of India

AYUSH FEDERATION OF INDIA

The AYUSH FEDERATION OF INDIA is a Doctors Association that brings together dedicated professionals from Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy to promote excellence in healthcare, professional growth, and public welfare.

Through unity, collaboration, and shared knowledge, we strive to strengthen the AYUSH system and serve society with compassion, integrity, and dedication.

Our Mission

To unite and empower AYUSH professionals across all five systems of medicine, fostering excellence in healthcare delivery, promoting evidence-based practice, and ensuring the highest standards of patient care through continuous learning and collaboration.

Our Vision

To establish AFI as the premier organization representing AYUSH professionals in India, creating a healthier society through integrated healthcare approaches, professional development, and community service while preserving and promoting India's rich traditional medical heritage.
